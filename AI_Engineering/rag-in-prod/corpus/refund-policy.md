# SwiftCart Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within **30 days** of delivery. Items must be
unused and in original packaging. Refunds are processed to the original payment
method within **5 to 7 business days** after we receive the returned item.

Digital goods and gift cards are **non-refundable**.

Orders above **10,000 rupees** require manager approval before a refund is
issued, which can add up to 2 extra business days.

To start a return, go to Orders → Select item → Request return. A prepaid
shipping label is emailed to you. Return shipping is free for defective items;
for change-of-mind returns a flat **99 rupee** label fee is deducted from the
refund.
