# Shipping & Delivery

Standard shipping is **free on orders over 499 rupees**; otherwise a flat 49
rupee fee applies. Standard delivery takes **3 to 5 business days**.

Express delivery (next business day) is available in select metro cities for
**149 rupees**. Orders placed after 2 PM ship the following business day.

We currently ship only within India. We do not ship to PO boxes.

Tracking links are emailed once the order leaves the warehouse. If tracking
shows no movement for 5 days, contact support to open an investigation.
