# Account Security

Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) from Settings → Security. We support
authenticator apps (TOTP) and SMS codes; authenticator apps are recommended.

Passwords must be at least **12 characters**. We lock an account for **15
minutes** after 5 failed login attempts.

If you suspect your account is compromised, change your password and revoke all
active sessions from Settings → Security → Active Sessions. Support will never
ask for your password or 2FA codes.
